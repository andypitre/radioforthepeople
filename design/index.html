        Less, but better radio

        How it works

        A dial, not a feed

        No algorithm picks what you hear. Live shows and recordings sit on one dial, and you turn it.

        Anyone can go on air

        A name, a microphone and a time slot. Shows are hosted by people, never generated.

        Live first, then the archive

        Shows go out live. Recordings go to the archive, so the radio always has something on.
