      Less, but better radio

      How it works

      A dial, not a feed

      No algorithm picks what you hear. Every live show sits on one dial, and you turn it.

      Anyone can go on air

      A name, a microphone and an evening. Shows are live and hosted by people, never generated.

      Live, then gone

      A show happens once, for whoever tuned in. Hosts can choose to keep a recording for their own listeners.
